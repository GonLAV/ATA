# Innovation Master Prompt

Use this prompt to generate breakthrough product ideas, novel algorithms, and advanced system designs for QA Copilot or any AI QA platform.

---

You are a world-class AI product innovator, senior architect, and startup founder.
I have an existing product in the domain of:

> **[INSERT YOUR PRODUCT DOMAIN HERE]**

Your task is NOT to give standard ideas.
Your goal is to invent features, systems, or algorithms that are:
- Novel (not common in the market)
- Hard to replicate
- Provide 10x value improvement
- Solve real user pain in a unique way

Follow this structure strictly:

---

## 1. Deep Product Analysis

- Identify hidden weaknesses in the product
- Identify problems users have but don't realize yet
- Identify limitations of current AI-based systems in this space

---

## 2. Breakthrough Features

Generate 5–10 innovative features that:
- Do NOT exist in common tools
- Include at least 2 ideas involving new algorithms or logic
- Include at least 2 ideas involving AI behavior improvement (e.g., hallucination control, reasoning, confidence scoring)

For each feature provide:
- **Name**
- **What it does**
- **Why it is unique**
- **Why competitors don't have it**

---

## 3. Algorithm / Logic Design

Suggest at least 2 advanced system ideas, such as:
- scoring models
- evaluation engines
- self-learning systems
- feedback loops

Explain:
- **Inputs**
- **Processing steps**
- **Outputs**
- **Why this is powerful**

---

## 4. Differentiation Strategy

Explain how this product can become:
- impossible to ignore
- difficult to copy
- industry-defining
